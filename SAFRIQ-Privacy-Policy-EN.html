Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how SAFRIQ handles information when you use the app.

1. Information we handle

SAFRIQ may handle account information, travel plans, ticket details, documents you choose to upload, hotel information, app settings, AI assistant conversations, reports, suggestions and support complaints. This also includes purchase details and transaction identifiers needed to validate subscriptions and question packs, and device information needed to deliver notifications. Location is used only when you enable features that require it, such as directions to an airport or place-based reminders.

2. Why we use this information

We use information to deliver the travel features you request, including saving trips, reading tickets, scheduling reminders, displaying flight status, storing documents, arrival services, maps, AI assistance and security. We do not use your documents to deliver targeted advertisements.

3. Private files and documents

Passports, identity documents, visas, insurance documents, tickets and hotel photos are uploaded only when you choose to upload them. Private files saved to your account are kept in account-specific private storage. Certain sensitive local trip details, hotel safe codes, saved hotel reminder locations, taxi safety details, luggage and taxi plate photos, and saved places are stored locally with encryption when supported by the device. When synchronizing flight status, we minimize what is sent to our server and exclude the passenger name, booking reference (PNR), barcode contents and ticket price from that server-side status copy. Non-sensitive offline copies are also limited to what is needed.

4. Artificial intelligence

When you use AI-assisted reading or chat, information necessary to fulfil your request may be sent to the AI provider operating the feature. We seek to minimize data sent and use on-device processing when available.

5. Locations and external services

SAFRIQ may use external services, including RevenueCat to validate store purchases and associate them with your account, as well as airline, airport, mapping, visa, communications and booking services. Account, product and transaction identifiers necessary for validation are sent to RevenueCat. If you use an external service, that provider’s privacy policy may also apply.

6. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers. We do not share your private files with other users unless you choose a specific sharing feature. Lost-and-found reports show only general details to authorized users and exclude private verification information or concealed contact numbers. Support suggestions and complaints are private to the owner or administrators. Trip-sharing links are designed to display basic trip information only, without passports, IDs, hotel or safe details, or personal location. SAFRIQ treats sharing links and companion pairing tokens as sensitive and encrypts them on-device when secure storage is available. Live family location sharing happens in WhatsApp at the user’s discretion; SAFRIQ does not read that shared location.

7. Notifications and location

The camera is requested when you choose to photograph or scan something. Photos are selected through the device picker without automatically granting access to the full library. Notifications and location features require relevant device permissions and in-app consent. Optional airport, hotel and border-area reminders may send an enter/exit event and its coordinates to the SAFRIQ server to deliver a reminder and prevent duplicates, without creating a continuous movement trail. You can disable the feature in SAFRIQ; if you cannot confirm that monitoring has stopped, revoke location permission in device settings.

8. Retention and deletion

The previous community forum has been removed and no longer accepts new posts. Any existing forum records remain subject to their applicable retention and deletion policy; removing the interface does not automatically erase other account or report records. You can permanently delete your SAFRIQ account in Settings. When deletion is confirmed, we remove account, trip, chat, private-file and associated family data from SAFRIQ servers and clear account-specific data from the device. Deleting your SAFRIQ account does not cancel a store subscription. Manage or cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions separately in the relevant store.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards, including private storage and authentication checks before access to private files. No online service can guarantee complete security; please avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about your account information, contact us at: safriq.app2030@gmail.com
